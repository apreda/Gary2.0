# Cash Rail Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The NFL marquee card on Home becomes the Cash Rail betting scoreboard at kickoff, fed by a settlement
engine that turns BDL plays plus frozen pregame prices into cashed bets.

**Architecture:** Two parts that meet at one row shape. Part A (iOS) renders a `CashRailBoard` (one row per game:
state + `hits` JSON) inside `HomeMarqueeTracker`'s live state, with a DEBUG demo timeline for the simulator.
Part B (Node) freezes prices before kickoff, settles plays into hits with a pure function, and a launchd worker
upserts one `live_boards` row per live NFL game every 15 seconds. The app polls that row.

**Tech Stack:** SwiftUI (iOS 17), XcodeGen; Node 20 ESM, Vitest; Supabase (PostgREST, SQL migrations);
BallDontLie NFL API.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-cash-rail-design.md` (mock: `.superpowers/brainstorm/68346-1793310397/agents/opus-1.html`)

## Global Constraints

- Only bets the game has already cashed appear; prices are the frozen pregame prices; no unders, no live odds.
- Price per bet = best price across books in the last snapshot before kickoff. Over/under markets use the over side.
- No sportsbook names on screen. No "…" anywhere (`.fitsOneLine()` / `.clipsWithoutEllipsis()`, design.md).
  No oval pills; actions are gold text with a chevron (`ALL HITS ›`).
- Minus signs render as U+2212 (`−115`), as in the mock.
- `$10 → $X`: X = 10 × decimal odds; whole dollars at $100 and up, else two decimals with ".00" dropped.
- Colors/type from `GaryColors` / `GaryFonts` (gold #C9A227, light gold #E8D48B, win #3FB950, Bebas display).
- Under the team names: `<spread> · GARY <pick>` (e.g. `GB −6.5 · GARY PACKERS −6.5`).
- Ladder rung strip appears only when 2+ rungs of that ladder have cashed.
- NFL only in this plan. Every other league's live hero stays exactly as it is.
- Commit with explicit pathspecs; never commit `ios/GaryApp/GoogleService-Info.plist` or `gary2.0/deno.lock`.

## Review Focus

1. A TD wiped out by penalty or replay: settlement re-runs over all plays each poll, so a hit can disappear;
   the card must drop it without crashing or animating a "new" hit. (Task B2 test, Task A2 test.)
2. Overtime: period 5 plays must still settle; the rail clamps OT landings to its right end. (B2, A3.)
3. Defensive and return TDs count for anytime TD; a passer's TD pass never does. (B2.)
4. No price snapshot for a game: the board shows the scoreboard and rail with zero hits, never a price. (B3.)
5. A non-NFL live hero renders the existing live view unchanged. (A4.)

### Task 6 (B2): Settle plays into hits

**Files:**
- Create: `gary2.0/src/services/cashRail/settle.js`
- Test: `gary2.0/tests/cashRail/settle.test.js` with fixture `gary2.0/tests/fixtures/cashRail/ne-chi-plays.json` (last week's 180 BDL plays, saved by the probe)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: B1 `Market[]`.
- Produces: `export function settleNfl({ markets, plays, game, garyPick }) -> { hits: Hit[], possession, situation, elapsed_s, clock, status }`
  with `Hit` = the A1 `CashRailHit` JSON shape (ladder markets of one player+propType fold into one hit with `rungs`).
  Elapsed = `(period-1)*900 + (900 - clockSeconds)`; OT periods continue past 3600. `when` = `Q2 4:30` / `END Q1` / `FINAL`.
  Scorer of a TD = the participant whose type is not `passer` (receiver, rusher, returner, interceptor, recoverer).
  Supported prop types: anytime_td (1+, 2+, 3+), first_td, receiving_yards, rushing_yards, passing_yards,
  rushing_receiving_yards, receptions, passing_tds, passing_completions, passing_attempts, rushing_attempts,
  interceptions (thrown), longest_reception, longest_rush, longest_pass, and the `_1q`/`_1h`/`_2h` windows of these.
  Game: total over cashes on the play that crosses it; spread and moneyline at `status_state == 'final'`.
  `garyPick = { type: 'spread'|'ml'|'total', team?, line? }` marks the matching game hit `gary: true`.

- [ ] Step 1 tests (NE @ CHI fixture, synthetic markets): first_td cashes for the first TD's scorer only; a receiver's
  60+ milestone cashes on the exact play that crosses 60 (assert `elapsed` and `when`); his 100+ does not cash if he
  ends at 87; the passer gets no anytime TD; re-running settle on the same plays returns deep-equal hits; dropping the
  TD play from the input removes that hit (Review Focus 1); a synthetic period-5 play settles with elapsed > 3600
  (Review Focus 2); a synthetic interception-return TD cashes anytime TD for the defender (Review Focus 3).
