## Football assessment — recommendation, not a prompt change

Both NFL and NCAAF scouts already attach recorded first-seen/current line
history through `oddsSnapshots.formatLineHistory`; different books are labeled
as not comparable movement. Both constitutions discuss short samples, but also
categorically say the market already knows and prices that uncertainty.
That assumption is too broad for Adam's requested independent assessment.

Recommended next implementation: a dated “what changed since last week?”
evidence section for **both** football leagues, reusing the existing history.
It should connect the previous game and opponent, performance behind the score,
actual roster/QB/coaching changes, the size and dates of the sample, and the
exact observed book/line/price changes. Use drive/box context only when sourced.
For college, distinguish a current team from its prior-season roster baseline.

An observed move can be justified, excessive, insufficient or inconclusive.
Do not create an automatic fade, home-team preference, regression formula or
confidence score. Winning an ugly game and playing at home are observations,
not proof of a mispriced ticket. A line move alone cannot identify public
overreaction or sharp-money activity. A quote captured after last week's game
cannot establish the pre-result expectation. If a genuine pre-game lookahead
quote is absent, say it is absent; never rename first-seen as the opener.

Replace the categorical market-pricing sentence with neutral permission to
investigate those observations and reach either conclusion at the actual price.
Keep the verdict Gary's own. This was discussion in Adam's current message;
**no NFL/NCAAF prompt or game-decision rule was edited in this change**.
June MLB and the 300-pick freeze remain untouched.
