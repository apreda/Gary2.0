> **When:** the day Northwest emails that the Ohio Articles are approved
> (they submit to the state by Aug 14; approval typically ~3–7 business days).
> **Where:** irs.gov → search "Apply for an EIN online" → IRS EIN Assistant.
> Online hours: Mon–Fri, 7 a.m.–10 p.m. ET. The EIN is issued INSTANTLY at the
> end — download the CP 575 confirmation letter PDF and save it to this folder.
> ⚠️ Only use irs.gov. Never pay a third-party "EIN filing service" — it's free.
