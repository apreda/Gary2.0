This Operating Agreement (this "Agreement") of **Gary A.I. LLC**, an Ohio
limited liability company (the "Company"), is entered into and shall be
effective as of the date the Company's Articles of Organization were filed
with the Ohio Secretary of State (the "Effective Date"), by its sole member,
**Adam Preda** (the "Member").

## Article I — Formation

1.1 **Formation.** The Company was formed as an Ohio limited liability company
by the filing of Articles of Organization with the Ohio Secretary of State
pursuant to the Ohio Revised Limited Liability Company Act, Ohio Revised Code
Chapter 1706 (the "Act").

1.2 **Name.** The name of the Company is **Gary A.I. LLC**. The Company may do
business under the name "Gary AI," "Gary," "BetWithGary," or such other trade
names as the Member may adopt.

1.3 **Registered Agent.** The Company's statutory agent in Ohio is Northwest
Registered Agent Service, Inc., 6545 Market Ave. North, STE 100, Canton, OH
44721, or such successor agent as the Member may appoint.

1.4 **Purpose.** The Company is formed for any lawful business purpose
permitted under the Act, including without limitation the development,
operation, and commercialization of software applications, websites, and
related media and analytics services.

1.5 **Duration.** The Company's existence is perpetual unless dissolved in
accordance with this Agreement and the Act.

## Article II — Member; Membership Interests

2.1 **Sole Member.** Adam Preda is the sole member of the Company and holds
one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interests.

2.2 **Limited Liability.** The Member shall not be personally liable for the
debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a
member, except as required by the Act.

2.3 **Additional Members.** Additional members may be admitted only upon the
written consent of the Member and upon such terms as the Member determines, at
which time this Agreement shall be amended and restated as a multi-member
operating agreement.

## Article III — Management

3.1 **Management by Member.** The Company is member-managed. The Member has
full and exclusive authority to manage the business and affairs of the Company
and to bind the Company, including without limitation: opening and maintaining
bank and payment-processing accounts; entering into contracts (including
platform agreements such as the Apple Developer Program License Agreement and
similar distribution agreements); hiring employees and contractors; acquiring,
licensing, and disposing of Company property (including intellectual
property); and borrowing money.

3.2 **Officers.** The Member may appoint officers or agents with such titles
and duties as the Member determines. Any such appointment does not, by itself,
convey any membership interest.

3.3 **No Fiduciary Conflict for Other Activities.** The Member may engage in
other business activities, whether or not competitive with the Company.

## Article IV — Capital; Distributions; Tax

4.1 **Capital Contributions.** The Member has contributed and may from time to
time contribute cash, property, or services to the Company as the Member
determines. No interest accrues on capital contributions, and the Member has
no obligation to make additional contributions.

4.2 **Distributions.** Distributions of cash or property shall be made to the
Member at such times and in such amounts as the Member determines, subject to
any limitations of the Act.

4.3 **Tax Classification.** So long as the Company has a single member, it is
intended to be disregarded as an entity separate from the Member for U.S.
federal income tax purposes. The Member may elect a different classification
(including S-corporation election) if the Member determines it advantageous.

4.4 **Records.** The Company shall maintain books and records adequate to
reflect its operations, and shall maintain funds in Company accounts separate
from the personal funds of the Member.

## Article V — Indemnification

5.1 The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Member, and any officer
or agent of the Company, from and against any loss, damage, or claim incurred
by reason of any act or omission performed in good faith on behalf of the
Company, except for loss, damage, or claim resulting from gross negligence,
willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law.

## Article VI — Dissolution

6.1 The Company shall dissolve only upon: (a) the written election of the
Member; or (b) any event requiring dissolution under the Act. Upon
dissolution, the Company's affairs shall be wound up, its assets applied first
to creditors and then distributed to the Member, and a certificate of
dissolution filed with the Ohio Secretary of State.

## Article VII — Miscellaneous

7.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Ohio, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

7.2 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing executed by
the Member.

7.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the sole operating agreement of
the Company and supersedes all prior understandings with respect to its
subject matter.

7.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the
remainder shall continue in full force.
