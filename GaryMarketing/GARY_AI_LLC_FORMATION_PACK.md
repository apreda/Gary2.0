## 1. DECISION: OHIO. Not Delaware. (researched Aug 11 2026)

| | Ohio (home state) | Delaware |
|---|---|---|
| Filing fee | **$99 once** | $110 |
| Annual report | **NONE — Ohio has no LLC annual report** | Annual franchise tax **$300/yr** |
| Registered agent | You, free — or ~$39/yr for privacy | Required in DE (~$50–300/yr) |
| Extra registration | None — you operate here | **Must ALSO foreign-qualify in Ohio (+$99 + 2nd agent)** because the business operates from Ohio |
| Ongoing cost | ~$0–39/yr | ~$400+/yr forever |
| When it wins | Solo founder operating business (you) | VC-track C-corps, complex multi-member ownership, Court of Chancery needs |

**Delaware would cost ~$400+/year forever and STILL require registering in
Ohio.** Its advantages (investor familiarity, chancery court) apply to raising
venture capital — if that day comes, you convert/reform then; forming in Ohio
now blocks nothing. Apple does not care which state: it cares the entity is
real and D-U-N-S-verified.

**Privacy answer (his ask):** you cannot hide *ownership* (and shouldn't try),
but you CAN keep your name/home address off the public filing: Ohio Articles
don't list members at all, and a commercial registered agent's address replaces
yours on the record. Northwest Registered Agent (~$39 first year) is the
standard privacy pick. That gets ~all the "different name on the record"
benefit he asked about, without Delaware's costs.
