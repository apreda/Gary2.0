### 1. What is free, and what is Winners?

> Gary's game picks and written reasoning, available player props, Hub, public record and private Book stay free. Winners is a separate reviewed shortlist of published tickets with its own record. During the launch preview, Winners is included without a purchase. You can check the current offer at https://www.betwithgary.ai/pricing.

Do not equate the whole free slate's record with Winners performance. Do not claim every scheduled game already has a published pick.

### 2. How does founding access work?

**Before the cutoff:**

> Winners is open for the launch preview until October 1, 2026 at midnight Eastern. Accounts created before that cutoff retain founding access to Winners. No purchase is needed for included access. Eligibility is tied to the account, not just an app installation. Create or sign in to your account at https://www.betwithgary.ai/account.

**After the cutoff, only once the authorized product owner verifies the transition:**

> Accounts created before October 1, 2026 at midnight Eastern retain founding access to Winners. Other accounts can choose an available Winners plan. Game picks, reasoning and Book remain free. Sign in with the account you used originally and open Your membership at https://www.betwithgary.ai/account to check your access.

Midnight means the start of October 1, not the end of that day. Do not replace the offer with “install before October,” “free this season,” an invented later expiry or a purchased lifetime-plan promise. If an individual's access looks wrong, route it privately to the authorized account/billing owner; do not promise an entitlement change or advise creating another account to obtain access.

### 3. Does 65% confidence mean a 65% chance of winning?

> No. Gary's confidence is a judgment attached to the analysis, not a calibrated probability or a guarantee. Read the reasoning and the opposing evidence alongside the call. The audit shows what actually happened within displayed confidence bands: https://www.betwithgary.ai/results/audit.

Do not turn a confidence label into betting instructions, guaranteed returns or a claim of predictive calibration.

### 4. Which record am I looking at?

> The public Results page is Gary's record. Your manual Book entries are private and self-graded; they do not count toward public rankings. Choices made with BET WITH GARY or FADE THE BEAR settle automatically and remain separately labeled. The headline game record, core-prop record and Winners record have different scopes, so compare the same dates and type of pick. Your Book is at https://www.betwithgary.ai/you and Gary's public results are at https://www.betwithgary.ai/results.

If asked about the headline exclusions, add:

> Core props are reported separately from game picks. Home Run and Touchdown fun picks are excluded from the headline record. Check the scope note beside the figure you are reviewing.

Do not call a user's manual entry independently verified or claim all kinds of results share one combined win rate. Do not promise that grades never change: the methodology allows documented corrections.

### 5. Did Gary place a bet when I clicked a button?

> No. Gary does not place real-money wagers, take deposits or connect to your sportsbook. Your Book records your decisions. BET WITH GARY and FADE THE BEAR record a choice within Gary; they are not sportsbook orders.

If the person reports an actual monetary charge or unauthorized activity, distinguish a subscription/billing issue from wager placement and route it privately to the authorized support owner. Do not investigate by asking for sportsbook credentials, full card details or banking access.

### 6. Where is the reasoning?

> Open https://www.betwithgary.ai/picks, choose the game, then open its full card. The written read appears below the call; some cards also have a “The full analysis” section. If today's call has not appeared, https://www.betwithgary.ai/archive contains dated examples. Those examples are historical, not current recommendations.

Use the person's actual public matchup URL when available. Do not send a private Book URL or a token-bearing account link as an example.

### 7. Why is my game's pick missing or still pending?

> A scheduled game and a published pick are different things. The board shows a pending status before a call is available and can show “No call posted” after a game starts. I can't promise a pick or an exact posting time for this game. You can check the board again or read a dated example in the archive: https://www.betwithgary.ai/archive.

For a reported discrepancy, request only the sport, teams, game date and public page path. Check the actual current website; do not say an outage exists just because a future game is awaiting its normal publication window. If there is a verified service issue, describe its observed scope and use the halt table below. Do not trigger generation, alter a pick or change the sports process from support.

### 8. I can't sign in / I forgot my password

> Open https://www.betwithgary.ai/account and use the same sign-in method you used before. If you joined with Google, choose “Continue with Google” and select that same Google account. For an email/password account, request a fresh reset link at https://www.betwithgary.ai/account/reset. If Gary asks you to confirm your email, check your inbox and spam folder for the confirmation message.

If the reset link is expired:

> Please request a fresh link at https://www.betwithgary.ai/account/reset and open the newest message. The reset screen does not confirm whether an email address has an account. If you still see an error, send the visible error wording and your browser/device type to support@betwithgary.ai. Don't send your password, a sign-in code or the reset link itself.

A form's success message is not proof of mailbox delivery or account existence. Do not ask for credentials, sign in as the user, reset access administratively or promise account merging. Route any account-specific investigation privately to the authorized owner. The website currently offers Google and email/password; do not invent another available sign-in button.

### 9. A result or factual detail looks wrong

> Please send the public page URL, the exact sentence or result label, and reliable supporting information to support@betwithgary.ai. The correction process is at https://www.betwithgary.ai/corrections. A losing pick is not by itself a factual error, but a wrong team, statistic, date or grade can be reported for checking.

Do not promise a change, refund or review deadline before the relevant owner checks the facts. Keep private Book contents out of public comments.

### 10. Is the latest app update available?

> I can confirm the website features at https://www.betwithgary.ai/picks. For the native app's current release status, we'll use the release owner's latest verified information rather than a planned date or a build number in our notes.

This draft intentionally contains no App Store status. The separate release owner supplies any final native availability statement; this website/marketing session must not inspect or change the app.
