## 5. Why no new competing pages were created

Spec §6.2 asks for one primary page per intent and for improving existing sport pages rather than creating competing pages with near-identical names. The reasons no further pages were added:

1. **The evergreen sport pages already own the "today" intent.** `/picks/<sport>` renders today's slate (`todayEST()`, 3 AM ET roll), and every published slate already has a dated permalink at `/picks/<sport>/<date>` in the game shard (4,330 URLs on 2026-09-16, `read_live.json`). A `/mlb-picks-today` page would compete with both. The fix was to make the sport page a real door: recent dated listings, the last slate with results on off days, and cross-links (Task 6).
2. **The only missing URLs were the two markets that searches treat as their own page.** Section 2 shows home-run and anytime-TD searches served by dedicated daily pages; Gary had none (`read_props.json` gap §6.3). The plan chose the spec's preferred paths once — `/props/home-runs` and `/props/touchdowns` — and built them from the existing feed, predicates and native cards (Task 3). No third lane exists because no third fun-lane market is published (`prop-lanes.ts`: `isLongShot = isMlbHomeRun || isNflAnytimeTdPick`).
3. **No NCAAF touchdown page.** The web lane definition is NFL-only (`web/lib/gary/prop-lanes.ts:5-16`; `tests/picks.test.ts:61` pins NCAAF `anytime_td` as core), the props record is defined the same way (`results.ts:165-167`), and the spec says "NFL anytime touchdowns". The backend stamps NCAAF scorer picks as the fun lane (`footballPropsDesk.js:52`), so this is a product decision for Adam, not something to infer (`critic.json` contradiction 3).
4. **No per-insight Hub pages.** Hub rows are not individually addressable and the critic's fix explicitly says not to add a new insights route unless Adam wants insights indexed separately (`critic.json` ranked gap 22). Task 9 adds anchors and links inside `/hub` instead.
5. **No `/winners` variant and no public results page for the fun lanes.** `/winners?date=` already canonicalises to `/winners` (`read_pages.json` Q8); a home run tally is prohibited by founder policy (`ledger.ts:28-31`, `/results/audit` copy), and any TD results block needs Adam's explicit call.

## 6. Why no guides were added (spec §6.9)

Spec §6.9 allows a small number of lasting guides only if they fill a real need and warns against generic articles to increase page count. The real questions in the section 2 results — what a home run or anytime-TD bet is, what to weigh (pitcher matchup, park, wind, red-zone role), how picks are graded, whether Gary is free, which sports are covered — are already answered on the pages people land on:

- **Per-sport guide on every sport page**: `SportGuide` (`web/app/picks/[sport]/page.tsx:41-76`) — "How Gary covers MLB/NFL/NCAAF" with "The research" (sport-specific research lens), "The call" and "The receipt", linking `/how-it-works`, `/data-sources`, `/results/<sport>` and the latest completed slate. Live text confirmed on `page_04_picks_nfl.txt` and `page_05_picks_ncaaf.txt`.
- **Props guide on `/props`**: "How to read Gary's player prop picks" (`web/app/props/page.tsx:162-191`) — the line, the reasoning, the result, plus the variance caveat and links to `/results/audit`, `/data-sources`, `/results`.
- **Lane explainers on the two new pages** (Task 3 `PROP_LANES.explainer`): "A home run pick is a bet that the named batter hits at least one home run in that game. Home run picks are long shots: they are published as picks, never counted in Gary’s game record, and are not part of Winners." and "An anytime touchdown pick is a bet that the named player scores a touchdown at any point in the game. Gary does not publish first-touchdown picks. Touchdown picks are long shots: published as picks, kept out of the core prop record, and not part of Winners." Each page also links yesterday's graded cards in `/archive/<date>`, all of today's props, the other lane and the sport page.
- **`/how-it-works`** with FAQPage JSON-LD (`web/app/how-it-works/page.tsx:14-19`; questions include "Is Gary free?", "What sports does Gary cover?", "Does Gary place bets?", "How is the track record calculated?"); it is the one prerendered, cache-HIT page in the live sample (`meta_report.txt`, `page_17_how_it_works`).
- **`/editorial-standards`, `/data-sources`, `/corrections`, `/results/audit`** already cover authorship, data categories, corrections and calibration.
- **The "what to consider" content lives in the picks themselves.** The live Sep 16 Hub carried "Gary Home Run Threats" with the batter-vs-pitcher and park reasoning in text (`page_07_hub.txt`), and every prop card ships its reasoning in the initial HTML (`read_props.json` §5–6). That is original, dated, per-pick content; a generic evergreen article would duplicate it with less value and would be the scaled-content pattern Google's spam policies describe.

Result: zero new guide pages. The existing explainers are now reachable from every primary page through the links added in Tasks 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9, and `llms.txt` lists the sport and lane pages (Task 5).
