# Founder-led launch videos to record

Prepared September 4. No video has been recorded, generated or posted. These scripts reopen the founder-video option for review. They do not create a faceless automated video program. Use the website, X, or another channel only after its gambling-content eligibility is resolved. TikTok's requirements are separate from Instagram's.

Use original phone footage and an actual current app or website screen. Keep the game/date visible. Record clean speech; licensed music is optional, and silence under narration is fine. Add accurate captions, keep them clear of platform controls, and end with one useful next step. Do not use broadcast clips, team logos, fake bet slips or profit claims.

## 1. At the game · about 25 seconds

| Time | Picture | Spoken words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Adam, phone in hand; original neutral sports setting | “Ever watch a game and wonder what Gary thinks about it?” |
| 5–12 s | Real screen: choose a supported matchup, open its published pick | “Find the game. Here’s his pick.” |
| 12–20 s | Scroll slowly into the actual written reasoning | “And here’s why he chose it. Read the matchup, then make your own decision.” |
| 20–25 s | Adam or clean product end frame | “That’s what I built Gary for. Try a game you care about.” |

End frame: “Find your game. See Gary’s pick. betwithgary.ai/picks · 21+ · Sports information. No wagers placed.” If using historical footage, label the date/example throughout the screen segment. Do not say “live” about an old pick.

## 2. The record includes the bad days · about 30 seconds

| Time | Picture | Spoken words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Adam on camera | “A screenshot of one winner doesn’t tell you much.” |
| 5–15 s | Actual Results screen, with the selected dates and scope visible | “Gary keeps the game picks and their results on the record. You can look through the wins and the losses.” |
| 15–24 s | One actual settled win and one actual settled loss from that window | “Start with the whole period, then read the original picks. Don’t judge him from one night.” |
| 24–30 s | Results destination | “The game record is here. Props are separate. The fun home-run and touchdown picks aren’t included.” |

Show only freshly verified figures from the declared window. No win-rate or ROI graphic is required. End note: “Past results do not guarantee future outcomes. 21+.”

## 3. Your own Book · about 30 seconds

Capture the website's Book for this version; use native-app footage only after the matching iOS update is released and its behavior is verified.

| Time | Picture | Spoken words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Adam | “I wanted a place to keep track of my own decisions, too.” |
| 5–15 s | Demo account: add a clearly labeled example pick, original odds, units and a note | “Your Book lets you save the pick, the price you took and why you chose it.” |
| 15–23 s | Show manual result entry and separate system-settled choices | “Record how it finished, then look back at what you were thinking.” |
| 23–30 s | Private manual Book and product destination | “It’s private by default. You don’t need to connect a sportsbook. It’s tracking, and it doesn’t place a bet.” |

Use a demo account with no real customer information. Label example data on screen and exclude it from marketing analytics. End frame: “Your Book. Your record. betwithgary.ai/you · 21+.”
