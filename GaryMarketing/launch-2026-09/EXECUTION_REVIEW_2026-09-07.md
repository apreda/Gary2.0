## What changed after review

**Publication recovery is implemented.** Previously, a root tweet could succeed before its reply or log failed, leaving the next scheduled run free to publish the root again. The new publisher reserves the exact game before X is called, saves the root receipt before attempting its reply, and repairs logs from confirmed receipts. Conditional state transitions prevent overlapping workers from attempting the same stage twice. The database serializes reservations against the existing 30-thread daily cap. Provider game IDs distinguish same-ticket doubleheaders and preserve identity across ticket/start corrections; ID-less games use exact normalized matchup/start identity. Legacy logs keep conservative ticket-text protection.

An unknown root/reply response is deliberately left unresolved and reported. This sacrifices automatic retry when acceptance is uncertain; it does not claim transactional exactly-once delivery across X and PostgreSQL. After the pregame deadline, recovery can repair the already-published root's log but cannot issue a late root or reply. Prepared reservations resume their original copy; unresolved reservations do not repeatedly consume model-selection attempts or crowd new games out of the queue. The existing scheduler, publishing hours and cadence are unchanged.
